Website privacy.

This page explains how this website works. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Browsing this website

The website does not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address, to provide and secure the hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

The enquiry builder

The enquiry builder creates a message in your browser. It does not upload your details to a website server or save them in browser storage. The website does not retain a copy of your enquiry.

When you select “Open WhatsApp” or “Open email”, your message is passed to the chosen service or app so you can review and send it. Those services have their own privacy practices. The WhatsApp link includes the message in its URL, which may remain in browser history. The copy button only writes your message to your clipboard when you select it.

Contacting Anastasia

If you send an enquiry by phone, email or WhatsApp, you share that information directly with Anastasia for discussing your care requirements. Please ask Anastasia directly about how enquiry and booking records are handled or to request access, correction or deletion.

Please do not include payment details, door codes or other sensitive information in the enquiry builder.
